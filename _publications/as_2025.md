This work applies an ensemble machine learning approach that featurizes LSMO thin-film surface morphology to capture nonlinear correlations with electronic and magnetic properties, classifying films into five representative types and demonstrating surface morphology as an efficient, non-invasive probe of the strongly correlated properties in ferromagnetic perovskite oxides.
