This paper proposes a unified in situ compression-artifact-removal framework that combines fully convolutional networks with scalable training, transfer learning, and experience replay, and shows on JPEG-compressed climate and nuclear reactor simulation data that a transfer-trained model incrementally updated during the reactor simulation substantially outperforms compressed sensing postprocessing (mean PSNR of 42.438 vs. 27.725).
