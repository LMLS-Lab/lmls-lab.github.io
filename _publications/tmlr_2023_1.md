This work proposes mL-BFGS, a momentum-based, block-wise Hessian approximation to L-BFGS that stabilizes stochastic convergence and distributes compute/memory across nodes, achieving both iteration-wise and wall-clock speedups over SGD, Adam, and other quasi-Newton methods in large-scale distributed DNN training.
