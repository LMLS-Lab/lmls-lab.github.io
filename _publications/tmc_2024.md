This work proposes a federated learning framework that lets memory-constrained clients participate by having each client train only as many consecutive output-side layers as its resources allow, provably converging near stationary points for non-convex problems and matching strong-client accuracy across CIFAR-10, FEMNIST, and IMDB while outperforming state-of-the-art width-reduction methods like HeteroFL and FjORD.
