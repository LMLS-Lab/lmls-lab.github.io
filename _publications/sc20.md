This paper generalizes the spread-out algorithm and adopts communication throttling to reduce the straggler and contention effects of the all-to-many personalized communication used in two-phase MPI-IO request redistribution, and shows on Cori and Summit that tailoring the communication kernel to different I/O patterns can improve end-to-end performance by up to 10x over default MPI-IO.
