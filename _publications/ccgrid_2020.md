This paper proposes a Bayesian-network model that exploits the spatiotemporal correlation of astronomical images to predict per-stage resource requirements in transient-detection pipelines like iPTF, and shows it identifies the most influential spatial and temporal features and achieves prediction errors close to the intrinsic random-variance limit.
