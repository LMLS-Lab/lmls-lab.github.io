This paper proposes a communication-efficient gradient-averaging algorithm for synchronous SGD that maximizes computation-communication overlap and is shown analytically to outperform traditional allreduce, achieving up to 2516x (VGG-16) and 2734x (ResNet-50) speedups on ImageNet with up to 8192 cores on NERSC's Cori supercomputer.
