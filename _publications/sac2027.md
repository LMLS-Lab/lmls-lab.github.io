AsyncSAM is a SAM variant that runs the perturbation step and the parameter update concurrently and asynchronously across multiple compute resources. It sizes the perturbation batch to fit the system, which removes SAM's extra computational cost. It keeps the same order of convergence as SAM and matches SAM's generalization in nearly the training time of SGD.
